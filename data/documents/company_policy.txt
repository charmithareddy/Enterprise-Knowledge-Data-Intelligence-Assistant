NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES
EMPLOYEE AND CUSTOMER POLICY DOCUMENT
2026

1. REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

Products must be returned in their original condition unless the product is defective.

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.

Digital products are generally non-refundable after download unless a technical issue prevents the customer from accessing the purchased product.

2. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Customers can contact the support team through the official support portal or registered email address.

Critical technical issues are prioritized and normally receive a response within 4 business hours.

3. EMPLOYEE REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.

Employees working remotely must remain available during their assigned working hours.

Remote employees must use company-approved devices and security tools when accessing company systems.

4. DATA SECURITY

Employees must not share company credentials with other individuals.

Sensitive company information must only be stored on approved company systems.

Employees must report suspected security incidents to the security team immediately.

5. ANNUAL LEAVE

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Employees should request planned leave at least five working days in advance whenever possible.

Emergency leave should be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible.

END OF DOCUMENT
