NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES
ENTERPRISE PRODUCT CATALOG, SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) & SUPPORT TIERS

1. ENTERPRISE PRODUCT CATALOG & SPECIFICATIONS
Nexora provides four primary enterprise data and AI products:
- Nexora CloudPlatform: Fully managed multicloud data lake and elastic compute engine. Offers auto-scaling, automated cross-region replication, and serverless querying.
- Nexora EnterpriseAI: Agentic AI orchestration platform integrating RAG, GraphRAG, text-to-SQL data querying, and machine learning model serving. Supports hybrid cloud and on-premise air-gapped deployments.
- Nexora SecuritySuite: Automated compliance posture management, zero-trust perimeter enforcement, runtime vulnerability scanning, and audit logging.
- Nexora DataHub: Unified metadata catalog, enterprise ETL pipelines, and bi-directional real-time data sync across Snowflake, Databricks, PostgreSQL, and BigQuery.

2. SERVICE AVAILABILITY (UPTIME) COMMITMENTS
Service availability is measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance announced 5 business days in advance:
- Enterprise Tier (Platinum): 99.95% monthly uptime guarantee (maximum allowable downtime: 21.9 minutes per month).
- Business Tier (Gold): 99.90% monthly uptime guarantee (maximum allowable downtime: 43.8 minutes per month).
- Developer Tier (Standard): 99.50% monthly uptime guarantee (best effort support).

3. SERVICE LEVEL CREDITS SCHEDULE
If Nexora fails to meet the guaranteed monthly uptime, customers on Enterprise or Business tiers are eligible for service credits:
- Uptime between 99.0% and 99.94% (Enterprise Tier): 10% credit applied to that month's subscription fee.
- Uptime between 95.0% and 98.99%: 25% credit applied to that month's subscription fee.
- Uptime below 95.0%: 50% credit applied to that month's subscription fee.
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the impacted calendar month with corresponding incident ticket references.

4. CUSTOMER SUPPORT TIERS & SEVERITY RESPONSE TIMES
- Critical Incident (Sev-1): System or production workload down with no available workaround. Response time: <30 minutes (24/7/365 dedicated engineering bridge).
- High Incident (Sev-2): Significant degradation in platform performance or core feature impaired. Response time: <2 hours (24/7/365).
- Medium Incident (Sev-3): Minor feature defect or non-critical operational impairment. Response time: <8 business hours.
- Low / General Inquiries (Sev-4): General questions, documentation requests, or minor feature enhancement suggestions. Response time: <24 business hours.
API Rate Limits for Enterprise Customers: Up to 10,000 requests per minute with guaranteed p99 latency under 250 milliseconds.
