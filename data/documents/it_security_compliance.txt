NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES
INFORMATION SECURITY, ZERO-TRUST & SOC2 COMPLIANCE STANDARD
OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVE & AUDIT SPECIFICATION

1. ACCESS CONTROL & IDENTITY MANAGEMENT
Nexora Technologies operates under a strict Zero-Trust Architecture.
All employee and contractor access to production infrastructure requires Hardware Security Key (FIDO2/WebAuthn) Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
SMS-based verification and email one-time passcodes are strictly prohibited for production access.
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and the Principle of Least Privilege are enforced across all cloud tenants.
Privileged administrator sessions expire after 4 hours of inactivity and require re-authentication with hardware biometric keys.
Employee access credentials must never be shared, hardcoded in repositories, or stored in unapproved password vaults.

2. DATA CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING TIERS
All company and customer data must be classified into one of four sensitivity tiers:
- Tier 1: Public - Marketing collateral, public documentation, open whitepapers. No encryption restrictions.
- Tier 2: Internal - Internal operating procedures, department wikis, general roadmaps. Requires TLS 1.3 in transit and standard tenant isolation.
- Tier 3: Confidential - Employee salary data, business financials, code repositories. Must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and restricted to authorized personnel.
- Tier 4: Restricted - Customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII), API tokens, database encryption keys, and customer operational datasets. Requires column-level envelope encryption, dedicated VPC isolation, and audited access logs retained for 7 years.

3. INCIDENT RESPONSE SEVERITY LEVELS & SLA PROTOCOLS
Security incidents must be reported immediately to security@nexora.internal. Severity classifications and SLAs are defined as follows:
- Severity 1 (Critical): Active data breach, unauthorized production database access, or total service downtime. Response SLA: 15 minutes. Mandatory executive notification to CISO, CEO, and Legal within 1 hour. Regulatory notification (e.g., GDPR/CCPA) within 72 hours.
- Severity 2 (High): Compromise of an individual administrative account or vulnerable dependency exposed to the internet. Response SLA: 1 hour. Remediation required within 12 hours.
- Severity 3 (Medium): Unpatched internal non-critical vulnerability or violation of data classification policy. Response SLA: 4 hours. Remediation required within 72 hours.
- Severity 4 (Low): Phishing attempt with no credential submission or suspicious network probe blocked by firewall. Response SLA: 24 hours. Reviewed during weekly security triage.

4. COMPLIANCE AUDITING & REGULATORY STANDARDS
Nexora maintains continuous compliance under the following enterprise certification frameworks:
- SOC 2 Type II: Annual independent third-party audit covering Security, Availability, and Confidentiality trust principles.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022: Global Information Security Management System certification.
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation): Full support for Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR) and Right to be Forgotten within 30 days.
- Disaster Recovery & Continuity: Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is 15 minutes; Recovery Time Objective (RTO) is 60 minutes for Tier 1 infrastructure.
Automated penetration testing is executed bi-weekly, supplemented by an annual external red-team engagement.
